Free Laptop Scheme for Students

Scheme Code: TN-DL-2026/001 | Financial Year: 2026-27

As part of the Government of Tamil Nadu's Digital India policy, free laptops are being provided to economically backward and academically outstanding students. Through this scheme, 15,000 students will benefit every year.
